## 📈 Key Findings

According to the analysis:

* **Adults (35–64)** generate the highest total profit, at approximately **16.32 million**.
* **Young Adults (25–34)** are the second-highest profit-generating group, contributing approximately **11.39 million**.
* **Youth (<25)** contribute approximately **4.37 million** in total profit.
* **Seniors (64+)** generate the lowest profit, at approximately **0.14 million**.
* Customers aged **25–64** account for the majority of total profit.
* **Bikes** are identified as the primary revenue driver.
* Accessories and clothing provide opportunities for cross-selling alongside bike purchases.
* High-volume accessories such as **water bottles, patch kits, and tire tubes** are identified as products for which adequate inventory should be maintained.

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis in the notebook:

1. Focus on **Bikes** as the primary revenue driver and maintain sufficient inventory to meet demand.

2. Use **Accessories and Clothing** as cross-selling opportunities through bundles and product recommendations with bike purchases.

3. Target customers aged **25–64** with personalized promotions and loyalty programs because these groups generate the majority of profit.

4. Maintain adequate stock of high-volume accessories such as **water bottles, patch kits, and tire tubes** to reduce the risk of stockouts.
